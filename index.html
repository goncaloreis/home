A one-hour call

We build businesses, and will tell you what we would do in yours.

Given

  • Every result is partly luck.

    Effort and talent shift the odds; they do not decide the outcome.

  • No one knows enough.

    Knowledge is partial, scattered and provisional, ours included.

  • Time runs out.

    Every choice is paid for in it, and none of it comes back.

What follows

Facts set no aim. Ours is to build businesses that last.

  • Trust no single result.

    A launch that worked may have been lucky, and one that failed may only have been early; no one can tell which from the result alone. So we treat a new price or a new hire as a test, and write down before it starts what we expect and what would change our mind.

  • Check what you can, and act on the rest.

    A decision that keeps being put off is usually waiting for a fact. Some facts can be had within a week, by calling customers or reading the contract, and we get those first. The rest will not arrive in time, so we decide without them and set a date to look again.

  • Keep every loss survivable.

    Any one attempt can fail on chance alone, so the outcome turns on how many good attempts a business gets, and the clock limits those. We count runway in attempts rather than months, and size each bet so that losing it leaves enough for the next. No single bet has to win.

A stalled business is usually breaking one of these.

From inside the business it is hard to see which, and more effort fixes none of them. Naming it is most of the work.

The call

One paid call, on one problem that will not move.

Bring it from a business you are building or backing. We take it back to its facts, work out what follows, and say what we would do in your place, and why.

The call lasts one hour.

To book, tell us what the business is, the problem in a few lines, and what has been tried.
