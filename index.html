We build businesses, and will tell you what we would do in yours.

Every business we’ve built has run into the same things. Some of what went well was luck, and so was some of what went wrong. No one knew enough, ourselves included. And every month spent finding out was a month gone. None of that can be changed. What to do about it depends on what you want, and we want businesses that last.

What we do about it

  • Trust no single result.

    A launch that sold out may have been lucky. One that flopped may only have been early. From the result alone, there’s no telling which. So we treat a new price or a new hire as a test, and write down before it starts what we expect and what would change our mind.

  • Check what you can, and act on the rest.

    A decision that keeps slipping to next month is usually waiting for a fact. Some facts can be had within a week, by calling customers or reading the contract again, and we get those first. The rest won’t arrive in time, and waiting for them costs more than they’re worth. We decide without them, and set a date to look again.

  • Keep every loss survivable.

    Any attempt can fail on chance alone, however good it is. The outcome turns on how many good attempts a business gets before the money or the patience runs out. We count runway in attempts rather than months, and size each bet so that losing it leaves enough for the next. No single bet has to win.

The call

One paid call, on one problem that won’t move.

It can be in a business you run or one you’ve backed. We work through it the way we work through our own, from what the results so far do and don’t show to what you can afford to lose. By the end, you’ll know what we’d do, and why.

The call lasts one hour. To book it, tell us what the business is, the problem in a few lines, and what’s been tried.
